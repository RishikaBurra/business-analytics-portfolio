## Featured Business & Data Analytics Projects

### 1. HR Attrition & Workforce Analytics Dashboard (MS Excel)
- **Business Objective:** Analyze turnover patterns across **160 employees** to uncover high-risk departments and retention drivers.
- **Tools Used:** MS Excel (Interactive KPI Cards, Department Slicers/Filters, Multi-Dimensional Breakdowns).
- **Key Findings:** Identified a **29.4% overall attrition rate** (47 exits), with **Finance (15 exits)** and **IT (11 exits)** showing the highest turnover compared to **R&D (3 exits, 11.2 yrs avg tenure)**.

### 2. Retail Store Performance & Customer Experience Analysis (MS Excel)
- **Business Objective:** Benchmark retail store execution across **61 mystery audit records, 25 cities, and 4 geographical zones** to identify customer journey gaps.
- **Tools Used:** MS Excel (Pivot Tables, Performer Segmentation, Regional Benchmarking).
- **Key Findings:** Calculated an overall score of **71.79**, uncovering a 10-point performance gap between **North (76.74)** and **South (66.61)** zones across upselling and objection handling.

### 3. Amazon Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)
- **Business Objective:** Track monthly sales revenue, product category performance, and regional trends across a **3-page interactive executive report**.
- **Tools Used:** Power BI (36 visuals, KPI cards, slicers, data modeling), Power Query, MS Excel.

### 4. Business SQL Queries Portfolio (SQL Server)
- **Business Objective:** Solve customer, transaction, product, and banking account analytics problems.
- **Tools Used:** SQL (`CTEs`, `ROW_NUMBER()` Window Functions, `INNER/LEFT JOINS`, `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`).

### 5. Academic Research Paper Presentation — IMI Kolkata
- **Business Objective:** Conducted audience sentiment and theme research across **2,083 unstructured YouTube comments** using **VADER Sentiment Analysis** and **Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA)**.
